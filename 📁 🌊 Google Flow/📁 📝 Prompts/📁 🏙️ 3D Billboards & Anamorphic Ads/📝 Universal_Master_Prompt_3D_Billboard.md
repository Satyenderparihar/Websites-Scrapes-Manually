`

### स्टेप 2 — BILLBOARD DESIGN ARCHITECTURE

- **Display Structure**: Create a huge high-resolution curved/corner LED billboard integrated into the architecture of the mall. The billboard should have a deep black/dark premium frame and a realistic LED screen surface.
- **Anamorphic Illusion**: The advertisement should be specifically designed to create a powerful **ANAMORPHIC 3D ILLUSION**.
- **Physical Extrusion**: The product should appear to physically break out of the screen and extend into the real-world space outside the billboard.

## 💥 Part 2: 3D Pop-Out Effect & Depth Layers

Make the hero product dramatically emerge from the billboard. Approximately **30–50% of the product** should visually extend beyond the physical boundaries of the screen. Parts of the product may overlap the billboard frame and appear to float several feet into the street.

### 5 Depth Layers of Physical Extrusion:

1. **Layer 1**: Background inside the billboard.
2. **Layer 2**: Product inside the screen.
3. **Layer 3**: Product partially crossing the screen boundary.
4. **Layer 4**: Hero product dramatically protruding outside the screen.
5. **Layer 5**: Small foreground elements coming toward the viewer.

> Add realistic shadows, reflections, atmospheric depth, contact shadows and perspective distortion so the product genuinely looks like it is physically coming out of the LED display.

### Product-Category Matching Visual Effects:

- **Food or Beverages**: Realistic liquid splashes, ingredients, steam, ice, crumbs, droplets or particles.
- **Cosmetics or Perfume**: Mist, fragrance particles, floating petals, liquid droplets or elegant particles.
- **Shoes or Fashion**: Fabric movement, dust particles, dynamic motion trails or floating accessories.
- **Electronics**: Energy waves, glowing particles, light streaks or futuristic interface elements.
- **Any Other Product**: Create tasteful, physically believable environmental effects that enhance the product without distracting from it.

## 📐 Part 3: Composition, Perspective & Lighting Rules

### Composition & Perspective:

- **Hero Focal Point**: The hero product must be extremely large and immediately visible. Keep the product as the central focus.
- **Street-Level Angle**: Use cinematic perspective with the camera positioned from street level looking slightly upward toward the billboard.
- **Foreshortening**: Create dramatic foreshortening so the product appears significantly larger as it comes toward the viewer.
- **Urban Context**: Include a realistic modern city environment around the mall: pedestrians, cars, storefronts, glass architecture, reflections, street lights and subtle urban activity.
- **Audience Engagement**: Some pedestrians should naturally look toward the billboard or record it with smartphones, emphasizing the spectacular 3D illusion.

### Lighting & Environment:

- **Lighting Quality**: Premium cinematic commercial lighting. Realistic LED screen illumination.
- **Reflections & Shadows**: Natural reflections on surrounding glass buildings. Physically accurate shadows.
- **Atmospheric Effects**: Subtle atmospheric haze, high dynamic range, photorealistic materials, ultra-realistic depth and perspective.

### Style & Critical Negative Rules:

- **Commercial Aesthetic**: Luxury global advertising campaign. Premium commercial photography. Photorealistic, cinematic, high-end 3D advertising.
- **Anamorphic Sensation**: Immersive Chinese/Tokyo-style 3D billboard illusion. The final image should look like a real photograph taken in a major Asian city where an enormous 3D billboard advertisement is physically protruding from the corner of a shopping mall.
- **⚠️ Critical Negative Rules**: Do NOT make it look like a normal flat billboard. The 3D pop-out illusion must be the main visual feature. Do not change the product branding. Do not invent a different product. Do not add unnecessary text. Do not add watermarks. Aspect ratio: 16:9.

## 🎥 Part 4: Universal Video Master Prompt (8 Seconds)

Create an ultra-realistic 8-second cinematic advertisement from the provided 3D anamorphic billboard image.

### 📋 Ready-to-Copy Gemini Video Setup Prompt:

`	ext
I want to generate an 8-second anamorphic video based on the master prompt. Please set up and preserve all product details.
`

### Camera & Physics Rules:

- **Camera Movement**: Realistic handheld cinematic camera movement from a bystander perspective at street level. Very subtle camera shake. Natural perspective.
- **Camera Prohibitions**: No rapid camera movements. No artificial zoom. No spinning camera.
- **Anamorphic 3D Effect**: Extreme depth, foreshortening, parallax, realistic occlusion, contact shadows, reflections, perspective distortion, volumetric depth, and realistic lighting interaction.
- **Frame Crossing**: The billboard frame should act as a physical boundary. The product must visibly cross that boundary.
- **Physics Accuracy**: All movement must obey realistic physics. Liquid must behave like real liquid. Particles must have realistic weight and momentum. The product must have realistic mass.
- **Lighting & Reflections**: Shadows and reflections must react correctly to movement. Realistic LED illumination, natural reflections on the mall facade, physically accurate shadows, realistic ambient city lighting, subtle atmospheric haze, HDR cinematic exposure.
- **Product Consistency**: The hero product must remain identical throughout all 8 seconds. Do not change shape, size, proportions, logo, branding, packaging, label, typography, colors, materials, textures, or distinctive features. No morphing, melting, deformation, or duplicate products.
- **Output Specifications**: Duration: 8 seconds. Aspect ratio: 16:9 widescreen. Ultra high detail, smooth motion.

## ⏱️ Part 5: 8-Second Cinematic Video Sequence Timeline

`	ext
🎬 8-Second Cinematic Anamorphic Video Sequence
⏱️ 0–2 Seconds (Street Establishing): Start with a realistic street-level view of the shopping mall and its giant corner LED billboard. The billboard initially appears normal and integrated into the building. The camera slowly pushes toward the billboard with subtle cinematic movement.
⏱️ 2–5 Seconds (The Pop-Out Emergence): The hero product begins moving forward from inside the billboard. It gradually crosses the physical boundary of the LED screen and into real-world space. Create strong anamorphic perspective and dramatic foreshortening. Supporting elements move naturally toward the viewer (liquid splashes, food particles, mist, dynamic trails).
⏱️ 5–7 Seconds (Maximum 3D Impact): The hero product reaches its maximum pop-out distance from the billboard. Small particles or droplets move past the camera lens creating strong foreground depth and parallax. Camera subtly moves backward or sideways to emphasize scale. Pedestrians naturally look and record with smartphones.
⏱️ 7–8 Seconds (Hero Settle & Final Shot): The product slowly settles back toward the billboard while maintaining the anamorphic illusion. End on a powerful hero shot where the product is still partially outside the billboard.
`
