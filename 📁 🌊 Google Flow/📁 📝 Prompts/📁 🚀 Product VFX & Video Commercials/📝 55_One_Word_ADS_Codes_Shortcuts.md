## 💡 How to Use & Real Talk Guide

`	ext
How to use: type a code, then your product. e.g. /packshot my cold-brew bottle on marble or /cheesepull loaded nachos, close up. Stack them for combos: /POV /unbox my snack box. Real talk: these aren't secret hidden commands. They're prompt shorthand — presets that signal a clear creative direction to the model. They work best when you add your subject, angle and lighting after the code, and they carry across ChatGPT Image, Higgsfield, Veo, Kling etc. with small tweaks.
`
