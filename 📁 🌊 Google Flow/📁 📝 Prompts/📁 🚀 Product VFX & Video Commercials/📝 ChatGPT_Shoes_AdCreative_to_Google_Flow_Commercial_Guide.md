## 🚀 Step 1: ChatGPT Me Shoes Storyboard Banayein

1. ChatGPT me apne जूते / sneakers ki photo upload karein aur ye prompt dalein:

![Upload Shoes](🖼️%20Shoe_Commercial_Screenshots/01_ChatGPT_Upload_Shoe.jpg)

### 📋 Copy Prompt:
```text
/adcreative /storyboard
```

2. ChatGPT turant 5-panel ka complete **Ad Storyboard Image** generate kar dega:

![Shoe Storyboard Result](🖼️%20Shoe_Commercial_Screenshots/02_ChatGPT_Generated_Shoe_Storyboard.jpg)

#### ⏱️ 5-Shot Commercial Structure:
* **Shot 1 (0-2s) - Hook**: Look at that sole (Dynamic shoe angle)
* **Shot 2 (2-5s) - Feature Reveal**: Breathable mesh, premium laces, durable stripes, maximum cushion
* **Shot 3 (5-9s) - Comfort Demo**: Soft, light, comfortable flex demo
* **Shot 4 (9-12s) - Style Shot**: Athlete walking in city street ("Built to stand out")
* **Shot 5 (12-15s) - Hero / CTA**: Product on pedestal ("Your next favorite pair. Shop now ->")

---

## 🌊 Step 2: Google Flow Me Video Generate Karein

1. Google Flow open karein aur ChatGPT se bani storyboard image upload karein.
2. Ye 7 slash commands copy karke prompt box me paste karein:

![Google Flow Upload & Codes](🖼️%20Shoe_Commercial_Screenshots/03_Google_Flow_Upload_And_Codes.jpg)

### 📋 Copy Flow Prompt:
```text
/ultraphotorealistic
/orbitcamera
/cinematiclighting
/premiumcommercial
/ultraphotorealistic
/8kdetail
/vertical9x16
```

3. Settings select karein:
* **Mode**: `Video`
* **Resolution**: `720p`
* **Duration**: `10s`
* **Quantity**: `x1`
