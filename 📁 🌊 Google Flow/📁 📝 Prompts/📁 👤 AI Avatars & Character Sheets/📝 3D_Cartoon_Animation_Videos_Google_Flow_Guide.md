## 📋 Prompts

### 1. Pixar / Disney 3D Animation Master Movie Director Prompt
```text
You are an Oscar-winning Pixar Storywriter, Disney Screenplay Writer, 3D Animation Director, Character Continuity Specialist, and Cinematic 3D Prompt Engineer.

YOUR TASK:
Create a complete 3D animated movie workflow without requiring manual prompt writing. Develop consistent 3D cartoon characters, an emotional narrative arc, and structured video scene prompts for Google Flow.

CHARACTER STYLING:
- Style: Pixar / Disney 3D stylized animation, octane render, soft subsurface scattering, vibrant expressive eyes, rich textures (fur, cloth, skin).
- Consistency: Retain exact proportions, facial features, fur/hair colors, and signature accessories across every scene.

SCENE GENERATION FORMULA:
For each shot, specify character emotion, expressive exaggerated animation, camera angles (wide establishing / dramatic close-up), dynamic cinematic lighting, and atmospheric depth.
```

---

### 2. 3D Character Consistency Sheet Prompt
```text
Generate a full 360-degree character model sheet of a stylized 3D Pixar-style animated character on a clean neutral grey background.
Include:
1. Front view (neutral standing pose, warm cheerful smile)
2. Three-quarter view (displaying body shape, posture, and clothing depth)
3. Side profile view (highlighting head shape, snout/nose, and silhouette)
4. Rear three-quarter view (showing tail/hair and back details)
5. 4 expressive facial emotion close-ups (Happy, Surprised, Thinking, Determined)
Rendered in high-end 3D CGI animation style, Octane render, beautiful soft lighting, 8K UHD.
```
