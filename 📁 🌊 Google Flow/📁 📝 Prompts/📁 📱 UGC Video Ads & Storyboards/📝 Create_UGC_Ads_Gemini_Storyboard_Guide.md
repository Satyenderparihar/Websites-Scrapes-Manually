## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open Google Gemini**: Launch the application or navigate to the interface.
2. **Select Create Image**: Press the Plus (+) button and select the image creation option.
3. **Upload Reference Images**: Attach character sheets, product photos, or brand assets to guide the style.
4. **Enter your Prompt**: Specify details for the storyboard (e.g., Create UGC Ads Story Board For Car).
