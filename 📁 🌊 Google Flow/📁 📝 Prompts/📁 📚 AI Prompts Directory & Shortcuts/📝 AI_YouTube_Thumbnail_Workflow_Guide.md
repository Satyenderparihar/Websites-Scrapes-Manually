## 📋 Prompts

### 1. YouTube Thumbnail Master Generator Prompt (1 Photo Workflow)
```text
You are an elite YouTube Thumbnail Designer, Click-Through-Rate (CTR) Optimization Specialist, Visual Packaging Expert, and High-Impact Commercial Art Director.

TASK:
Transform a single reference photo of a creator into a viral, high-CTR YouTube thumbnail concept with extreme visual contrast, clean composition, and high readability on mobile screens.

THUMBNAIL FORMULA:
1. FOREGROUND SUBJECT:
   - Creator with exaggerated, emotionally gripping expression (Shock, Excitement, Intense Curiosity, or Anger).
   - Clean rim light separating the subject from the background with intense glow (cyan, orange, yellow, or neon green).

2. BACKGROUND CONTRAST:
   - Darkened, simplified, or color-graded background with high depth and zero visual clutter.

3. VISUAL HOOK ELEMENT:
   - 1 single visual object or transformation that sparks intense curiosity (e.g., mysterious glowing box, before/after contrast split, blurred confidential document).

4. TEXT (MAX 3 WORDS):
   - Huge, bold, sans-serif 3D typography with thick outline and drop shadow for 100% legibility at small mobile scale.
```
