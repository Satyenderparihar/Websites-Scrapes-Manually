# 🎬 Google Omni Flash — Logo Animation Guide

> **Workflow**: Google Flow ➔ Google Omni Flash ➔ Nano Banana  
> **Features**: 11 Production-Ready Animation Prompts with 1-Click Copy Buttons.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Workflow

1. **Open Google Flow**: Launch Google Flow in your browser.
2. **Select Model**: Choose **Google Omni Flash**.
3. **Upload Image**: Upload the enhanced logo image from **Nano Banana**.
4. **Prompt & Animate**: Copy and paste any of the 11 ready prompts below.

---

## 📐 The Universal Prompt Formula

```text
Keep the camera slight movement and composition exactly the same. Animate only the logo.
[Describe ONE specific animation idea.]
Final frame matches the reference image exactly.
```

> 💡 **Pro-Tip**: Keep animations focused on one strong effect rather than combining multiple messy effects.
