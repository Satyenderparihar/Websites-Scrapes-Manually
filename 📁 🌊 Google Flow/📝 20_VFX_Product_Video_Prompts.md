## 💡 1. Giant Saree Rooftop Model
- **Best For**: `Perfume, phone, beauty, fashion, luxury products`

### 🖼️ Image Generation Prompt:
```text
A breathtaking, ultra-photorealistic cinematic shot of a stunning, authentically human Indian woman sitting casually and comfortably on the front rooftop edge of a modern, small-to-medium city building. She possesses lifelike skin texture, subtle pores, natural warmth in her complexion, and fine stray hairs gently catching the breeze. Captured from a high-to-low angle, looking downward. Right beside her sits the uploaded product, scaled to match her giant height, sharing perfect physical grounding, realistic ambient shadows, and reflections that blend seamlessly into the environment. Her beautiful saree is custom-designed with the product’s gradient colors and logo in a premium pattern, featuring highly realistic fabric physics, natural folds, and draping heavily with gravity. The building is just tall enough to emphasize her towering, majestic scale against the city skyline. Below, a bustling Indian intersection (chauraha) teems with visible cars, bikes, and pedestrians to ground the scale. She maintains warm, engaging, and confident eye contact with the camera, displaying a subtle, genuine micro-expression. Lighting: soft natural daylight with subsurface scattering on her skin, crisp contrast, and realistic shadows. Mood & Style: raw photorealism, cinematic, giant scale, heroic perspective. Camera Setup: high-to-low angle front downward tilt, 85mm lens, 8k resolution, shot on 35mm film aesthetic.
```

### 🎬 Image-to-Video Animation Prompt:
```text
Animate the uploaded image with a static camera. The giant Indian female model remains poised, maintaining direct eye contact with a gentle, confident smile. Subtle wind moves her saree and fine hair strands naturally. Down below, cars, motorbikes, and pedestrians move smoothly through the city intersection in realistic daylight traffic. The product remains perfectly grounded beside her, with realistic ambient reflections. Add subtle cloth motion, soft environmental movement, and natural city activity while preserving photorealism.
```
