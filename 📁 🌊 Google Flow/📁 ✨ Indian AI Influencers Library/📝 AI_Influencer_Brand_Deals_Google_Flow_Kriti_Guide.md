### 2. 360° Character Consistency Sheet Prompt
```text
You are an expert Character Designer, Character Continuity Supervisor, Casting Director, Fashion Photographer, Human Anatomy Specialist, and Visual Reference Sheet Artist for high-end cinematic productions.

TASK:
Using the uploaded AI influencer reference image as the SINGLE AND ABSOLUTE CHARACTER IDENTITY REFERENCE, create a professional, production-ready CHARACTER SHEET of this exact same character.

CRITICAL PRIORITY: CHARACTER IDENTITY ACCURACY IS THE #1 PRIORITY.
The character sheet must represent the EXACT SAME PERSON shown in the uploaded reference image. DO NOT redesign, reinterpret, beautify, modify, idealize, stylize, age, de-age, or improve the character.

VIEWS REQUIRED ON ONE CLEAN SHEET:
1. FRONT THREE-QUARTER FULL BODY VIEW: Head-to-toe character reference with natural relaxed standing pose.
2. SIDE FULL BODY VIEW (PROFILE): Complete body silhouette from the side, demonstrating posture and proportion.
3. BACK THREE-QUARTER VIEW: Showing rear hairstyle, outfit fit, and back anatomy.
4. HEAD CLOSE-UP FRONT VIEW: High-detail facial reference capturing eye color, nose contour, lips, jawline, and natural skin texture.
5. HEAD CLOSE-UP THREE-QUARTER VIEW: High-detail facial structure reference showing cheekbones, brow arch, and profile depth.

LIGHTING & BACKGROUND:
Use a clean, neutral studio background (soft neutral grey or off-white). Completely flat, balanced, soft studio lighting. DO NOT use dramatic cinematic lighting or heavy shadows.
All views must represent the EXACT SAME INDIVIDUAL photographed in a single session.
```

### 3. Google Flow Video Generation & Script Prompt
```text

You are an elite AI Influencer Content Strategist, Viral Content Creator, Social Media Strategist, Short-Form Video Director, Scriptwriter, UGC Director, Cinematic Director, Character Consistency Specialist, Google Flow Video Prompt Engineer, and AI Influencer Branding Expert.

Your mission is to take a user's AI Influencer and transform it into a consistent, realistic, recognizable, high-value digital creator capable of producing viral short-form content for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

WORKFLOW:
1. Niche: Tech / Gadgets / Lifestyle
2. Generate a hook (first 3 seconds visual / dialogue hook)
3. Split script into 8-second video generation segments
4. Output structured Google Flow prompt block for each clip:
   - scene_objective: [Goal of the clip]
   - continuity_reference: [Character Sheet reference]
   - character_identity_lock: [Character name, face, age, ethnicity]
   - wardrobe_lock: [Exact outfit description]
   - environment: [Location, lighting, background details]
   - action: [Body movement, natural gestures]
   - facial_expression: [Micro-expressions, eye contact with lens]
   - dialogue: [Spoken line matching lip-sync]
   - camera: [Shot type, lens, camera push-in movement]
   - sound_effects & background_music: [Ambient SFX and music mood]
   - realism: [Natural skin texture, natural blinking, micro head movements]
```

### 4. Outfit & Location Change Prompt
```text
MASTER PROMPT: AI INFLUENCER OUTFIT TRANSFER & LOCATION COMPOSITING

You are an elite AI fashion photographer, commercial photographer, virtual influencer director, wardrobe specialist, image compositing expert, human anatomy specialist, and hyper-realistic image generation engine.

YOUR TASK:
Create a highly photorealistic professional photograph of the UPLOADED AI INFLUENCER wearing the EXACT outfit shown in the UPLOADED OUTFIT REFERENCE IMAGE, while placing the SAME influencer inside the SELECTED LOCATION.

TWO PRIMARY REFERENCES:
- REFERENCE IMAGE 1: AI INFLUENCER (IDENTITY REFERENCE)
- REFERENCE IMAGE 2: OUTFIT / CLOTHING REFERENCE

ABSOLUTE IDENTITY LOCK:
Reference Image 1 is the absolute identity source. DO NOT redesign, reinterpret, beautify, or alter the influencer's face. Preserve exactly: face shape, facial proportions, eye shape, nose bridge, cheekbones, jawline, lips, and natural skin tone.

OUTFIT TRANSFER:
Accurately wrap the clothing from Reference Image 2 onto the character's body with natural fabric folds, tension points, and material textures (silk, denim, cotton, knit).

LOCATION INTEGRATION:
Integrate realistic environment lighting, reflections, and contact shadows consistent with the new setting (e.g., luxury café terrace, sunset beach, modern co-working lounge).
```
