## 📸 Step-by-Step Process with Video Screenshots

---

### 🔹 Step 1: ChatGPT Me Dress Ki Image Upload Karein

ChatGPT app open karein aur attachment (`+`) button par click karke apni dress ki photo upload karein:

![Step 1 - Upload Dress](🖼️%20Video_Screenshots/01_ChatGPT_Upload_Dress.jpg)

---

### 🔹 Step 2: Storyboard Prompt Type Karein

Dress image ke sath ye exact prompt enter karein:

![Step 2 - Type Prompt](🖼️%20Video_Screenshots/02_ChatGPT_Prompt_Typed.jpg)

```text
Create a story board image for my video edit cuts every second for Photoshop
```

---

### 🔹 Step 3: ChatGPT Se Generated 10-Panel Storyboard

ChatGPT turant 10 cuts (har second 1 cut) ka visual **VIDEO EDIT STORYBOARD** bana kar dega:

![Step 3 - Storyboard Result](🖼️%20Video_Screenshots/03_ChatGPT_Generated_Storyboard.jpg)

#### ⏱️ Storyboard 10-Shots Breakdown (1 Cut Every Second):
* **Header**: `VIDEO EDIT STORYBOARD`
* **Timeline Bar**: `10 SECONDS | 10 CUTS | EVERY 1 SECONDS | Ps PHOTOSHOP EDIT`

| Timecode | Shot Name | Visual Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `0 - 1s` | **Shot 1 - Full Dress [Intro]** | Full dress wide establishing shot on mannequin |
| `1 - 2s` | **Shot 2 - Upper Body Close-up** | Bodice embroidery and jacket cut |
| `2 - 3s` | **Shot 3 - Sleeve Detail** | Sleeve floral zari embroidery detail |
| `3 - 4s` | **Shot 4 - Skirt Flare** | Pleated skirt flare and border hem |
| `4 - 5s` | **Shot 5 - Fabric Flow** | Flowing silhouette and fabric movement |
| `5 - 6s` | **Shot 6 - Neckline & Cut** | Precision neckline styling and tailored cut |
| `6 - 7s` | **Shot 7 - Embroidered Pattern Detail** | Shimmering sequins and fine threadwork |
| `7 - 8s` | **Shot 8 - Angled View** | 45-degree angled perspective |
| `8 - 9s` | **Shot 9 - Close-up Texture** | High-detail fabric weave and texture |
| `9 - 10s` | **Shot 10 - Final Full View** | Grand full-length glamour reveal |

---

### 🔹 Step 4: Google Flow Me Storyboard Upload Karke Prompt Dalein

Google Flow open karein, ChatGPT se bani Storyboard image ko upload karein, aur ye prompt type karein:

![Step 4 - Google Flow Prompt](🖼️%20Video_Screenshots/04_Google_Flow_Upload_And_Prompt.jpg)

```text
Create a ugc cinematic video for professional video according to this story board sequence
```

---

### 🔹 Step 5: Exact Google Flow Settings Set Karein

Google Flow me exact ye settings select karein:

![Step 5 - Google Flow Settings](🖼️%20Video_Screenshots/05_Google_Flow_Settings.jpg)

* **Mode**: `Video`
* **Model**: `Omni 1.1 Flash`
* **Aspect Ratio**: `9:16`
* **Resolution**: `720p`
* **Duration**: `10s`
* **Quantity**: `x1`
* **Credit Usage**: `Generating will use 16 credits`
