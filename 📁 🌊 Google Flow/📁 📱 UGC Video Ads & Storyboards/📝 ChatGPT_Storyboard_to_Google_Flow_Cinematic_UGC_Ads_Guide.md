## 🚀 Step 1: ChatGPT Me Dress Upload & Storyboard Prompts

ChatGPT app open karein aur attachment (`+`) button par click karke apni dress ki photo upload karein:

![Step 1 - Upload Dress](🖼️%20Video_Screenshots/01_ChatGPT_Upload_Dress.jpg)

### 📋 Ready-to-Copy Prompt 1: Quick Storyboard Prompt (Tutorial Default)
> *Dress image upload karne ke baad ye prompt paste karein:*

```text
Create a story board image for my video edit cuts every second for Photoshop
```

![Step 2 - Type Prompt](🖼️%20Video_Screenshots/02_ChatGPT_Prompt_Typed.jpg)

### 📋 Ready-to-Copy Prompt 2: Master Detailed Storyboard Grid Prompt
> *Agar aapko aur zyada detailed aur accurate 10-panel grid chahiye, to ye master prompt copy karein:*

```text
Create a detailed 10-panel video edit storyboard image for my video edit cuts every second for Photoshop based on the uploaded dress image.

Header: VIDEO EDIT STORYBOARD
Specs: 10 SECONDS | 10 CUTS | EVERY 1 SECONDS | Ps PHOTOSHOP EDIT

Shot Breakdown:
- Shot 1 (0-1s): Full Dress [Intro] - Establishing wide view of dress on mannequin in an opulent palace room with warm ambient lighting
- Shot 2 (1-2s): Upper Body Close-up - Bodice embroidery, jacket lapels, and neckline fit
- Shot 3 (2-3s): Sleeve Detail - Macro focus on intricate sleeve floral zari threadwork and cuff embroidery
- Shot 4 (3-4s): Skirt Flare - Pleated skirt flare and bottom hem border details
- Shot 5 (4-5s): Fabric Flow - Silhouette profile and soft fabric movement
- Shot 6 (5-6s): Neckline & Cut - Detailed tailored cut and neckline styling
- Shot 7 (6-7s): Embroidered Pattern Detail - Shimmering sequins and delicate craftsmanship
- Shot 8 (7-8s): Angled View - Dynamic 45-degree angle luxury showcase
- Shot 9 (8-9s): Close-up Texture - Tactile micro-texture of fabric under soft lighting
- Shot 10 (9-10s): Final Full View - Grand final complete outfit glamour reveal
```

### 📋 Ready-to-Copy Prompt 4: Master Production Director Video Prompt (High Quality)
> *Google Flow me cinematic 4K commercial quality ke liye ye detailed prompt copy karein:*

```text
Create a UGC cinematic video for professional video according to this story board sequence. High fashion luxury commercial video, 10 seconds total duration, transitioning through all 10 storyboard shots with precise 1-second cuts:

- Shot 1 (0-1s): Grand palace room establishing shot with dress on mannequin, soft warm candlelight, chandeliers, and elegant bokeh.
- Shot 2 (1-2s): Smooth match-cut to upper body bodice embroidery and tailored jacket.
- Shot 3 (2-3s): Macro camera punch-in on sleeve floral zari threadwork with sparkling sequin shimmer.
- Shot 4 (3-4s): Pan down across pleated skirt flare and border hem.
- Shot 5 (4-5s): Gentle fabric movement with realistic cloth physics and soft flow.
- Shot 6 (5-6s): Close-up on tailored neckline and layered fabric collar.
- Shot 7 (6-7s): Extreme macro focus catching light on shimmering hand-stitched sequins.
- Shot 8 (7-8s): Dynamic 45-degree angled tracking shot.
- Shot 9 (8-9s): High-definition fabric weave texture and luxury silk/chiffon touch.
- Shot 10 (9-10s): Grand final full-length beauty reveal with subtle camera pull-back.

Pacing & Style: Seamless rhythmic cuts every 1 second, photorealistic cinematic lighting, 8K UHD commercial grade, authentic luxury fashion advertisement.
```

## 🚫 Step 5: Google Flow Negative Prompt

```text
blurry, distorted face, bad anatomy, deformed mannequin, floating garments, glitch, low resolution, warped text, stuttering cuts, cartoon, CGI look, oversaturated colors, abrupt jump cuts, flickering artifacts, low bitrate
```
