# 👗 ChatGPT Storyboard to Google Flow UGC Cinematic Video

> **Workflow**: Dress Image ➔ ChatGPT (Storyboard) ➔ Google Flow (Video)  
> **Category**: [📁 📱 UGC Video Ads & Storyboards](../📁%20📱%20UGC%20Video%20Ads%20&%20Storyboards/)

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: ChatGPT me Storyboard Banayein

1. **Upload**: ChatGPT me apni dress ki image upload karo.
2. **Type Prompt**:
```text
Create a story board image for my video edit cuts every second for Photoshop
```

### 📋 ChatGPT Output: Video Edit Storyboard (10 Seconds | 10 Cuts)
* **Title**: `VIDEO EDIT STORYBOARD`
* **Specs**: `10 SECONDS | 10 CUTS | EVERY 1 SECONDS | Ps PHOTOSHOP EDIT`

| Timecode | Shot Name | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `0 - 1s` | **Shot 1 - Full Dress [Intro]** | Full dress establishing wide view on mannequin |
| `1 - 2s` | **Shot 2 - Upper Body Close-up** | Upper body bodice fit and jacket lapel |
| `2 - 3s` | **Shot 3 - Sleeve Detail** | Sleeve embroidery and cuff detail |
| `3 - 4s` | **Shot 4 - Skirt Flare** | Flowing skirt flare and bottom hem border |
| `4 - 5s` | **Shot 5 - Fabric Flow** | Silhouette motion and fabric drape |
| `5 - 6s` | **Shot 6 - Neckline & Cut** | Neckline design and tailoring |
| `6 - 7s` | **Shot 7 - Embroidered Pattern Detail** | Shimmering embroidery, sequins, and threadwork |
| `7 - 8s` | **Shot 8 - Angled View** | 45-degree angled view of the outfit |
| `8 - 9s` | **Shot 9 - Close-up Texture** | Close-up fabric micro-texture and weave |
| `9 - 10s` | **Shot 10 - Final Full View** | Final complete dress hero reveal |

---

## 🌊 Step 2: Google Flow me Cinematic Video Banayein

1. **Open**: Google Flow open karo.
2. **Upload**: ChatGPT se bani Storyboard image upload karo.
3. **Type Prompt**:
```text
Create a ugc cinematic video for professional video according to this story board sequence
```

### ⚙️ Exact Google Flow Settings
* **Mode**: `Video`
* **Model**: `Omni 1.1 Flash`
* **Aspect Ratio**: `9:16`
* **Resolution**: `720p`
* **Duration**: `10s`
* **Quantity**: `x1`
* **Credits**: `Generating will use 16 credits`
