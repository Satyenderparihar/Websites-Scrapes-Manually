## 🚀 Quick Overview & Viral Workflow (सिर्फ 2 Steps में Video तैयार)

1. **Step 1 (ChatGPT)**: अपनी ड्रेस या किसी भी प्रोडक्ट की फोटो ChatGPT में अपलोड करें और Storyboard Grid Prompt डालें। ChatGPT तुरंत 10 कट्स (हर सेकंड 1 कट) का कम्पलीट **Video Edit Storyboard** जनरेट कर देगा।
2. **Step 2 (Google Flow)**: Google Flow खोलें, Storyboard फोटो अपलोड करें, और Video Director Prompt डालकर `Generate` पर क्लिक करें। Google Flow स्टोरीबोर्ड के हर कट को 1-1 सेकंड पर सीमलेसली एनिमेट करके 10-सेकंड की अल्ट्रा-सिनेमैटिक UGC ऐड वीडियो बना देगा।

## ⚙️ Recommended Google Flow Generation Settings

| Parameter | Recommended Value | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mode** | `Video` (with image reference) | Enables image-to-video animation based on uploaded storyboard |
| **Model** | `Omni 1.1 Flash` | Fast, sharp multi-cut temporal coherence and realism |
| **Aspect Ratio** | `9:16` (Vertical) or `16:9` (Cinematic) | `9:16` for Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts / TikTok Ads |
| **Resolution** | `720p` / `1080p` | High definition clarity with crisp embroidery details |
| **Duration** | `10s` | Matches the 10-shot storyboard (1 cut every 1 second) |
| **Multiplier** | `x1` | Uses 16 credits per run for maximum efficiency |

## 📌 Pro-Tips for Maximum Viral Engagement & High ROAS

1. **Rhythm Sync with Trending Audio**: 1-second cuts are engineered specifically for trending beat-drops on Instagram Reels and TikTok. Pick an upbeat 120-128 BPM soundtrack and sync each cut directly to the beat!
2. **Lighting Consistency**: Notice how the warm ambient candlelight and opulent palace room stayed consistent across all 10 shots. Always specify the environment in both ChatGPT and Google Flow prompts to maintain background continuity.
3. **No Fluff Intro**: The 0-1s shot acts as the instant visual hook. Ensure the most visually striking element is shown in the first frame to stop users from scrolling away.
