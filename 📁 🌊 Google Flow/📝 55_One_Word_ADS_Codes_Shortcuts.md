ADS CODES 

55 one-word prompt shortcuts for AI ad & product visuals 

How to use: type a code, then your product. e.g. /packshot my cold-brew bottle on marble or /cheesepull loaded nachos, close up. Stack them for combos: /POV /unbox my snack box. Real talk: these aren't secret hidden commands. They're prompt shorthand — presets that signal a clear creative direction to the model. They work best when you add your subject, angle and lighting after the code, and they carry across ChatGPT Image, Higgsfield, Veo, Kling etc. with small tweaks.

PRODUCT & PACKSHOT 

/packshot Clean studio hero shot — product on seamless background, crisp commercial lighting. /hero Dramatic low-angle hero frame with the product as the undisputed star of the shot. /float Product floating mid-air with its ingredients / elements orbiting around it. /splash Product hit by a liquid splash, frozen at peak — energy + freshness. /explode Exploded view — packaging and ingredients flying apart in a clean layout. /macro Extreme close-up. Texture, grain, crunch and detail fill the frame. /flatlay Top-down flatlay of the product styled with props on a surface. 

/mockup Slap your label onto a photoreal bottle / box / pouch mockup. 

/gradient Product on a bold saturated gradient — punchy, scroll-stopping. 

/glow Backlit halo / rim-light glow around the product for a premium aura. 

FOOD & BEVERAGE MONEY SHOTS 

/snack Appetizing snack close-up — crunchy texture, warm hunger-trigger lighting. /pour Slow-mo pour shot — sauce, syrup or drink streaming in. 

/steam Hot food with visible rising steam. Fresh-off-the-stove feel. 

/drip The melt / drip money shot — cheese, chocolate or honey running down. /sizzle Sizzling pan or grill with oil pop and heat shimmer. 

/cheesepull The classic stretch shot — cheese, caramel or gooey pull. 

/bite First-bite reveal — cross-section of the inside at the moment of the bite. /crush Ingredient crush / crumble in motion — spices, nuts, biscuit shatter. /freshpick Raw ingredients styled farm-fresh — dew, greens, natural light. 

/garnish Final plating beauty shot — the garnish landing, chef's-finish look.

POV & UGC — CREATOR-STYLE ADS 

/POV First-person view — a hand holding or using the product, viewer inside the moment. /UGC Casual creator-shot ad. Phone-camera realism, authentic, un-polished. /unbox POV unboxing reveal — the lid comes off, product revealed. /haul Creator haul spread — several products laid out show-and-tell style. /review Talking-head UGC review frame — person + product to camera. /grwm Get-ready-with-me routine that naturally features the product. /handheld Natural handheld motion, slight shake — reads as real, not an ad. /desk Product dropped into a lived-in desk / kitchen lifestyle setting. 

TRANSFORMATION & REVEAL 

/beforeafter Split before / after frame — same angle, only the result changes. /expose Cutaway reveal — slice the product open to show what's inside. /xray See-through view of the layers, fill or ingredients under the surface. /transform Morph from raw ingredient to finished product in one frame. 

/reveal Dramatic reveal from darkness or box — spotlight hits the product. /timelapse Ingredient-to-dish timelapse feel across a sequence of frames. /scale Miniature-world illusion — product turned giant, tiny people around it. 

MOOD, LIGHT & STYLE 

/luxe Luxury editorial treatment — premium, expensive, magazine-grade. /minimal Clean minimal Scandinavian styling — space, calm, one clear subject. /moody Dark, moody, dramatic lighting for a bold premium mood. 

/bright Bright airy daylight — fresh, clean, feel-good commercial look. /neon Neon-lit night mood — vibrant, urban, energetic. 

/vintage Retro / vintage ad aesthetic — grain, warm tones, old-print feel. /pastel Soft pastel palette — cute, friendly, gift-able styling. 

/bold High-contrast, high-saturation, loud graphic energy. 

/cinematic Filmic color grade, shallow depth, wide-frame commercial look. /festive Seasonal / celebration styling — confetti, warmth, occasion energy. 

SOCIAL & CAMPAIGN FORMATS 

/carousel Multi-slide Instagram carousel ad layout — one idea per slide. /thumbnail High-click thumbnail frame — product + bold focal point.

/story Vertical 9:16 story-ad frame with room for a hook up top. /poster Bold poster ad with clear space for a headline. /billboard Wide outdoor billboard hero composition — big, simple, legible. /banner Web / display banner layout with copy space beside the product. /grid 3x3 Instagram grid product spread — cohesive feed block. /lifestyle Product living in an aspirational real-life scene. /compare Side-by-side vs-the-other-guy comparison framing. /testimonial Customer-quote ad frame — face, quote, product together.
