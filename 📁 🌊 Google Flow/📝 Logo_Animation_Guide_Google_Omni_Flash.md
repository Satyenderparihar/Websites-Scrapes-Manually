Static logos को animate करने की exact workflow — Google Omni Flash के साथ

Step 1 — Animate the Logo

Open Google Flow.

Select Google Omni Flash.

Upload the enhanced image from Nano Banana.

Now use one of the prompts below.

Step 2 — Prompt Formula

हर prompt इसी structure को follow करता है:

Keep the camera slight movement and composition exactly the same. Animate only the logo.

[Describe ONE animation idea.]

Final frame matches the reference image exactly.

Tips

Keep prompts short and simple.

Focus on one animation idea per prompt.

The better your reference image, the better your animation.

If the first generation isn't perfect, regenerate it. Omni often gives much better results after a few tries.

Avoid describing unnecessary details. Let Omni focus on the animation instead of understanding a long prompt.
