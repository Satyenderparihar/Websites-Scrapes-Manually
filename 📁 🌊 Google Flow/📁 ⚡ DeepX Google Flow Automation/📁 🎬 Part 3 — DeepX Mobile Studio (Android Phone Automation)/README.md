## 🛠️ Complete Step-by-Step Mobile Setup Guide

### Step 1: Install Termux & Antigem Android Engine
1. Download **Termux** from [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/com.termux/) (avoid Google Play Store version as it is deprecated).
2. Install the **Antigem Native Android Engine** to run Antigravity seamlessly on mobile:
   - Visit: [🐙 GitHub: Santoshkurmi/antigem](https://github.com/Santoshkurmi/antigem)
   - Follow our dedicated guide: [📝 Antigravity on Android Setup Guide](./📝%20Antigravity_on_Android_Mobile_Antigem_Guide.md) or watch the companion video: [📺 Mobile Setup Tutorial](https://youtu.be/uZJPMTKVs_U) (8:00).

### Step 2: Install Kiwi Browser & Load DeepX Extension
1. Install **Kiwi Browser** from the Google Play Store.
2. Download and extract [`deepx-mob-studio`](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1mvd8uRonft0PrUNMMhP6RgVPy5VQ2Vfk) or use the unpacked extension directory from this repository.
3. In Kiwi Browser, go to `kiwi://extensions` (or tap the 3 dots menu ➔ Extensions).
4. Turn on **Developer mode**.
5. Tap **+ (from .zip/.crx/.user.js)** or **Load unpacked** and select the DeepX extension folder.
6. Open [Google Flow](https://flow.google.com) in a new tab in Kiwi Browser and sign in to your Google Account.
7. Tap the 3 dots menu and check **Desktop site** for the Google Flow tab.

### Step 3: Link DeepX Mobile Studio to Antigravity
1. In Termux, navigate to your project directory.
2. Ensure the DeepX Flow bridge MCP server is registered and running.
3. Start your session in Antigravity. It will detect the local bridge and confirm `ready: true`.

### Step 4: Generate Your First Complete Mobile Video
Simply send your video idea to Antigravity on your phone:
```text
Make a 9:16 vertical stickman video about "Why Procrastination Happens" in Hindi using DeepX Mobile Studio.
```
- Antigravity crafts the script and breaks it into visual beats.
- Cartesia AI generates the voiceover.
- DeepX extension injects prompts into Google Flow in Kiwi Browser without you touching the screen.
- Mobile rendering pipeline compiles the final 1080p MP4 ready to upload directly to YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels!

## 💡 Pro Tips for Mobile Creators

1. **Keep Kiwi Browser Active in Split-Screen or Background**: Prevent Android from putting Kiwi Browser to sleep by disabling battery optimization for Kiwi and Termux.
2. **Desktop Site Mode**: Always keep Google Flow in Desktop Site mode in Kiwi Browser for optimal canvas rendering.
3. **Daily Free Loop**: Use Google Flow's free daily regeneration and Cartesia's 20,000 free characters/month to create 30+ shorts per month completely free of cost.
4. **Instant Upload**: Since the final video renders directly in your phone's storage, you can open YouTube Studio or Instagram directly and publish immediately!
