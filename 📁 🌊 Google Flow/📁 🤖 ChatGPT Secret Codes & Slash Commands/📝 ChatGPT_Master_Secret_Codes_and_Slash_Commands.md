## 💡 How to Use Slash Commands & Stacking Protocol

ChatGPT slash commands (`/command`) are prompt shorthands that instantly set the output length, tone, complexity, visual layout, or analytical perspective without writing lengthy paragraphs.

```text
/command [your query, text, topic, or code]
```

**Example:**

```text
/eli5 /bullets Quantum Computing
```
