## 🎬 Step 2: 5-Shot Commercial Storyboard Result

ChatGPT turant ye 5-panel storyboard sheet generate kar dega:

![Shoe Storyboard Result](../📁%20🌊%20Google%20Flow/📁%20🚀%20Product%20VFX%20&%20Video%20Commercials/🖼️%20Shoe_Commercial_Screenshots/02_ChatGPT_Generated_Shoe_Storyboard.jpg)

* **Shot 1 (0-2s) - Hook**: Look at that sole
* **Shot 2 (2-5s) - Feature Reveal**: Breathable mesh, premium laces, durable stripes, maximum cushion
* **Shot 3 (5-9s) - Comfort Demo**: Soft, light, comfortable
* **Shot 4 (9-12s) - Style Shot**: Athlete walking in city street
* **Shot 5 (12-15s) - Hero / CTA**: Product on pedestal ("Your next favorite pair. Shop now ->")
